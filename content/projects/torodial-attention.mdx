## System

Linear attention geometries penalize the far end of long contexts by construction. Toroidal attention reimagines the context window as a 3D toroidal structure — circular wrapping like HDD tracks to kill edge bias, depth stacking like platters with sparse cross-depth fusion — with blockwise computation for long contexts and an FFT approximation path. Self-flagged highly experimental; the evaluation claims in the repo stand or fall with its test suite, not with this page.
