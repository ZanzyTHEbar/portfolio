## Hypothesis

The cited patent family investigates N-doped carbon-nanofoam catalytic material for fuel-cell applications.

## Constraints

- Fuel-cell operating conditions bound catalyst evaluation.
- The patent record describes catalytic material and separate fuel-cell elements.

## System
