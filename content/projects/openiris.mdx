## Strange Idea

EyeTrackVR documents OpenIris as its official ESP32 eye-tracking firmware.

## Hypothesis

On ESP32 targets, the firmware streams eye data through Wi-Fi, UVC, and ESP32-S3 USB while accepting OTA updates.

## Constraints

- ESP32 target support
- Wi-Fi and USB streaming
- OTA delivery

## System

OpenIris is EyeTrackVR's official ESP32 firmware and is archived. OpenIris-ESPIDF is its ESP-IDF rewrite with C/C++ firmware modules for cameras, Wi-Fi, UVC, REST and serial commands, and Python setup tools. The ESP32-S3 supports USB streaming of eye data to a PC and OTA updates.

## Evidence

- [EyeTrackVR README](https://github.com/EyeTrackVR/EyeTrackVR/blob/main/README.md): identifies OpenIris as EyeTrackVR's official ESP32 firmware.
- [OpenIris](https://github.com/EyeTrackVR/OpenIris): archived EyeTrackVR firmware.
- [OpenIris-ESPIDF](https://github.com/EyeTrackVR/OpenIris-ESPIDF): ESP-IDF rewrite.
