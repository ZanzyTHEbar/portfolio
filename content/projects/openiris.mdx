## Strange Idea

EyeTrackVR documents OpenIris as its official ESP32 eye-tracking firmware.

## Hypothesis

On ESP32 targets, the firmware streams eye data through Wi-Fi, UVC, and ESP32-S3 USB while accepting OTA updates.

## Constraints

- ESP32 target support
- Wi-Fi and USB streaming
- OTA delivery

## System

OpenIris is EyeTrackVR's official ESP32 firmware and is archived. OpenIris-ESPIDF is the active ESP-IDF rewrite, updated 2026-09-08. It supports ESP32 targets with Wi-Fi and UVC streaming, and the project provides Python setup CLI tools. The ESP32-S3 supports USB streaming of eye data to a PC and OTA updates.

## Reality

Public project material documents OpenIris in EyeTrackVR and does not identify the author's role. Both repositories publish releases.

- Latency and throughput measurements remain open.
