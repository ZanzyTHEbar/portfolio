## System

ThothNetwork is a real-time IoT backend in Go. It uses hexagonal architecture, NATS and libp2p networking, device management with digital twins, sub-second streaming, and X.509/JWT security. It began as a decentralized battery-management backbone. The project identifies multi-agent tasks as a potential general-purpose use.
