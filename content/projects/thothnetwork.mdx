## System

ThothNetwork is an experimental Go backend for IoT device management and is not ready for production use. Its hexagonal architecture uses NATS and libp2p networking, device twins, and X.509/JWT security. It began as a decentralized battery-management backbone and has also been used for decentralized multi-agent tasks.
