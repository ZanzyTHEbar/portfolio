## System

BRHP, Boltzmann Recursive Hierarchical Planning, is an OpenCode plugin for persistent planning sessions. Its `/brhp` command and TUI sidebar read sessions from `.opencode/brhp/brhp.db`. It models work as scopes, nodes, and validation signals, then records frontier selection and validation snapshots for inspection.
