## System

BRHP, Boltzmann Recursive Hierarchical Planning, is an OpenCode plugin. Its `/brhp` command and TUI sidebar use sessions stored in `.opencode/brhp/brhp.db`. It models work as scopes and nodes with validation signals. Frontier selections and validation snapshots are recorded in the session.
