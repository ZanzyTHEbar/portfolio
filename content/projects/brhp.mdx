## System

Long-running agent work fails in planning before it fails in code: disposable chat notes lose the thread across turns, and nothing records whether a plan ever converged. BRHP (Boltzmann Recursive Hierarchical Planning) is an OpenCode plugin that makes planning a first-class state — a `/brhp` slash command plus TUI sidebar over planning sessions persisted in `.opencode/brhp/brhp.db`, modeling work as a graph of scopes, nodes, and validation signals with temperature-controlled frontier selection and deterministic validation snapshots.
