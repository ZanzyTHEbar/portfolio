## System

Bank accounts and crypto wallets produce transactions outside Firefly III. Firedragon is a weekend-built Go importer. It pulls wallet and bank transactions through unified APIs such as Enable Banking and imports them into Firefly III for unified tracking. The project is self-declared unstable, unsuitable for production, and experimental.
