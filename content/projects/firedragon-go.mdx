## System

Transactions scatter across crypto wallets and bank accounts while the ledger sits empty waiting for CSV uploads. Firedragon is a weekend-built Go importer that pulls wallet and bank transactions — via unified APIs like Enable Banking — into Firefly III for unified tracking. Self-declared unstable and not for production; an experiment in collapsing manual import toil, kept honest about its state.
