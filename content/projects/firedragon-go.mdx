## System

Firedragon is a weekend Go importer for Firefly III. It retrieves bank and cryptocurrency transactions through unified APIs, including Enable Banking, and imports them into Firefly III. It is not for production.

- [ZanzyTHEbar/firedragon-go](https://github.com/ZanzyTHEbar/firedragon-go): Firefly III transaction importer.
