## System

mzi simulates programmable hexagonal Mach-Zehnder interferometer meshes with NumPy transfer matrices. It uses on-chip-inspired SPSA and adjoint training. Phase noise, thermal crosstalk, loss, and fabrication variation are modeled. The repository reports convergence with power conservation under its simulated conditions.
