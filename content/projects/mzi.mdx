## System

mzi simulates programmable hexagonal Mach-Zehnder Interferometer meshes. It uses NumPy transfer matrices and on-chip-inspired SPSA and adjoint training. The simulator includes phase noise, thermal crosstalk, loss, and fabrication variation. The repository reports convergence with power conservation under its simulated conditions.
