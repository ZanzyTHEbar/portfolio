## System

Photonic accelerators promise ultra-low-power AI, but only if training survives the hardware's own noise. mzi is a simulator for programmable hexagonal Mach-Zehnder Interferometer meshes — numpy transfer matrices, on-chip-inspired training (SPSA plus adjoint methods), realistic phase noise, thermal crosstalk, loss, and fabrication variation — reporting that the mesh still converges with power conserved and graceful degradation.
