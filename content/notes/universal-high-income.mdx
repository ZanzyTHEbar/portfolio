This opinion argues that AI systems can concentrate ownership of output, making distribution central to welfare.

Wages link production and livelihood. Under concentrated ownership, they are an insufficient sole link. The note treats the link between welfare and billable work as a design choice.

Universal Basic Income proposes a floor. It depends on fiscal capacity and political legitimacy. The note describes redistribution after surplus concentration as fragile.

Universal High Income calls for high living standards through pre-distribution at creation. Relative to the local median, it includes covered essentials, shock absorption, savings, upskilling, and bargaining power.

UHI uses multiple instruments: wages, dividends, services, capital income, portable benefits, profit-sharing, and credits.

The cited post attributes the phrase "Universal HIGH INCOME" to Musk in April 2026. The post does not document adoption. The note argues that distribution design constrains shared prosperity.
