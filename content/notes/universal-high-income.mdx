AI abundance shifts the political question from production to distribution. Models, compute, and platforms can concentrate ownership of output. The question becomes who can claim that output.

Wages have linked production and livelihood. Under concentrated ownership, they may no longer serve as the sole link. Tying human welfare to a shrinking share of billable tasks is a design choice.

Universal Basic Income proposes a humane floor. It depends on fiscal capacity and political legitimacy. Post-hoc redistribution of an already-concentrated surplus is fragile by construction.

Universal High Income calls for claimable high living standards through pre-distribution near the point of creation. Relative to the local median, it means covered essentials, shock absorption, savings, upskilling, and bargaining power.

UHI uses multiple instruments: wages, dividends, services, capital income, portable benefits, profit-sharing, and credits.

The cited post attributes the phrase "Universal HIGH INCOME" to Musk in April 2026. It is an example of the framing, not evidence of its adoption. The note argues that distribution design is the binding constraint on shared prosperity.
